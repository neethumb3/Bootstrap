What do you know about TCS as a company?
Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is a multinational information technology (IT) services, consulting, and business solutions organization headquartered in Mumbai, India. It is a subsidiary of the Tata Group (majority-owned by Tata Sons) and ranks among the largest IT services and consulting providers in the world.
Who is the CEO of TCS?
The Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and Managing Director of Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is K. Krithivasan
Why is TCS so famous?
Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is famous because it is one of the world's largest, most valuable Quora IT and consulting services companies, and a core financial engine for the prestigious Tata Group.
