How do I cancel a Flipkart order?
Open the Flipkart Help Centre, go to My Orders, select the specific item, and tap Cancel (available as long as the item has not been shipped out yet).
How do I track my Flipkart order?
Open the Flipkart app or website, go to the My Orders section under your account, select the specific item, and view its live tracking status
How do I return a product?
Open My Orders, select the delivered item, click Return, choose your reason for returning, and pick a pickup slot.
